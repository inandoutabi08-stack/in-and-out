How it works

Simple steps, for clients and for workers.

For clients

  1. Find a gig

    Search by category, city or price.

  2. Check the profile

    Read about the worker, their skills and their verification status.

  3. Agree on the details

    Talk about the job, the time and the price before you start.

  4. Get it done

    Meet in person or work online, then leave an honest review.

For workers

  1. Create your profile

    Add your skills, languages and city.

  2. Post a gig

    Describe what you offer and set your price.

  3. Get requests

    Clients write to you. You choose who to work with.

  4. Do the job

    Do great work and build your good name.

In this demo, payments, ID checks and calls are not active yet. Their buttons show “Coming soon”.

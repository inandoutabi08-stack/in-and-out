Stay safe

Simple rules that protect both clients and workers.

Safety rules and legal texts are not final. DRAFT: needs lawyer review.
📍

Meet in public places

For in-person jobs, first meet in a public, well-lit place or bring a trusted person.

📞

Tell someone

Tell a family member or friend where you are going, with whom, and when you expect to return.

💬

Keep talking on the platform

Agree on the job in writing here. Be careful if someone asks to move to another app straight away.

💳

Do not pay strangers in advance

Do not send money outside the platform. Safe payments will come later.

🔒

Protect your personal data

Never share passwords, card codes or SMS codes. Honest people do not ask for them.

🛑

Trust your feeling

If something feels wrong, stop, leave and report it. You never have to continue.

Report a problem

If someone makes you feel unsafe, tell us. A report form will be added in a later step.

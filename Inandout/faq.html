Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions.

What is In&Out?
A website where workers offer paid gigs, online or in person, and clients hire them.
Who can use it?
Women and men can both use In&Out, as clients or as workers.
Is it free?
Prices and fees are not decided yet. This is a demo.
Can I pay on the website?
Not yet. Online payments and safe payment holding are marked “Coming soon”.
How do I know someone is real?
Each profile shows a verification status. In this demo, all profiles are sample data and nobody is really verified.
Which languages are available?
English, Russian and Uzbek. You can switch at any time at the top of every page.
What if I have a problem?
Read the safety page and contact us. We will add a report form later.
